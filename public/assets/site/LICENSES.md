# Site Walk 3D assets

All models here are converted and trimmed copies of the packs below. Each pack is released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain); attribution is not required. Credit is kept here anyway.

| Pack | Author | Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3D House Construction Site (LowPoly) | Majadroid (Maik Hoffmann) | https://majadroid.itch.io/3d-house-construction-site | CC0 | crane, trucks, office containers, portable toilet, cargo container, skips, planks, boxes, barrel |
| Low Poly Construction Pack | Anker's Assets | https://ankersassets.itch.io/low-poly-construction-pack | CC0 | helmet, wheelbarrow, cement mixer, cones, barriers, fence panels, pallets, blocks, bricks, scaffolding, clipboard, skip, work light, steel, pipe, toolbox, fuel tank, ladder, cement bags, radio |
| Universal Base Characters (Standard) | Quaternius | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters | CC0 | crew and player bodies |
| Universal Animation Library (Standard) | Quaternius | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library | CC0 | idle, walk, jog, talking, sitting, working animations |

Rebuild with `npm run assets -- <folder with the extracted packs>`; review with `node scripts/assets/contact-sheet.mjs out.png`.
